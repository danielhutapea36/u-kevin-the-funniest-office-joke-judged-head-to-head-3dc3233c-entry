## The line

> The trust fall taught me to trust my colleagues — it was the only thing on the office calendar that ever ended on time.

The joke is in the turn: the setup sounds like a sincere lesson in trust from team-building day, then the punchline reframes it — the trust was earned by punctuality, the one thing no meeting on the office calendar ever manages. It turns on something anyone who has worked in an office knows (nothing ends on time), surprises instead of stating a complaint plainly, and lands the punchline last.
